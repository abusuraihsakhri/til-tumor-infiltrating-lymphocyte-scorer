Method and scope

Stromal TILs (%) = 100 × TIL-occupied stromal area / total eligible intratumoral stromal area. The tool performs arithmetic on supplied annotations; it does not determine the validity of the annotated areas.

  • Assess the stromal compartment within invasive tumor borders, averaging across the assessed tumor rather than selecting hotspots.
  • Include lymphocytes and plasma cells; exclude granulocytes, necrosis, crush artefact, biopsy-site change, DCIS-associated stroma, and normal structures as applicable.
  • Report the continuous percentage. No universal high/low cutoff, clinical urgency classification, or treatment recommendation is derived.

Methodology: International TILs Working Group (2014) and International Immuno-Oncology Biomarkers Working Group (2017).

All calculations and file processing are performed locally in your browser. Do not enter identifiable patient information. Research and education only; not validated for standalone clinical decisions.
